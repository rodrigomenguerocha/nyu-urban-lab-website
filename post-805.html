The Economist: As Spain prepares to vote, its Socialist prime minister sets out the case for continuity

March 24, 2024

Climate ChangeImpact Development NewsRenewable EnergySustainable Development

In an op-ed for The Economist, Spanish Prime Minister Pedro Sánchez outlines the achievements of his government in steering Spain towards economic resilience and sustainable development amidst global challenges like the pandemic and climate change. He emphasizes Spain's economic recovery, job creation, and extensive reforms in labor, pensions, and education, alongside a significant pivot towards a green and digital economy. Sánchez highlights Spain's leadership in renewable energy and its ambition to become Europe's industrial hub for clean energy, supported by substantial investments in solar and wind power. The piece advocates for continuity in these policies to ensure Spain's modernization and leadership in addressing global challenges, positioning the upcoming general election as a pivotal moment for the country's future direction.
