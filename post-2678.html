Case Study: Crosstown Concourse (Memphis, Tennessee)

July 30, 2024

Case StudyCulture Led DevelopmentCulture Led Development

Summary:

Crosstown Concourse, located in Memphis, Tennessee, is a mixed-use development that has transformed a historic Sears building into a vibrant vertical urban village. The project began with the vision of revitalizing the abandoned 1.5 million-square-foot structure, which had sat vacant since the early 1990s. Through a collaborative effort involving local artists, community leaders, developers, and the City of Memphis, the redevelopment project aimed to create a space that fosters cultural, economic, and social growth. Today, Crosstown Concourse houses a variety of tenants, including residential apartments, office spaces, retail shops, restaurants, healthcare facilities, and educational institutions. The project is renowned for its focus on arts and culture, with dedicated spaces for exhibitions, performances, and creative work. Crosstown Concourse has become a hub for community engagement and innovation, significantly contributing to the economic and cultural vitality of the surrounding area.

The district's impact on the local economy is significant. It attracts thousands of visitors annually, generating substantial economic activity that supports local businesses. The influx of visitors and the success of local businesses have helped revitalize the area, making it a sought-after destination for both residents and tourists. The district also provides valuable resources and support for artists and cultural organizations, ensuring that the creative community continues to thrive. The success of Crosstown Concourse is largely due to the collaboration between various stakeholders, including local government, real estate developers, and arts organizations. The City of Memphis has played a pivotal role by providing funding and policy support, while developers have contributed by integrating affordable cultural spaces into new developments. Arts organizations and community groups actively participate in planning and programming, ensuring that the district remains a vibrant and inclusive cultural hub.
