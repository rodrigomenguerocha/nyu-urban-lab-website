Net Zero Cities

See related publications ↗︎Upcoming events ↗︎
NYU Urban Lab studies urban and rural systems that will lead to net zero cities. Net Zero Cities as a category for us include:
  • Renewable energy generation in urban and rural environments
  • Macro- and Micro-grid infrastructure
  • Greenhouse Gas Emission (GHG) reduction strategies.
  • Building systems technology including passive house, modular construction strategies, smart buildings.
  • Transportation systems for passenger and freight movement including land, air, and maritime. This includes focus on mass transportation systems and maritime and blue highway initiatives.
  • Regenerative infrastructure.
  • Food supply and food system design.
  • Urban resilience in the face of extreme heat, sea level rise, storm surge, and seismic activity.
