NYT: What Does ‘Unabated’ Mean, Anyway?

March 26, 2024

Climate ChangeImpact Development News

The article delves into the significance and complexities surrounding the term "unabated" in the context of fossil fuels and climate negotiations at the COP28 summit. Here's a summary of the key points covered:

  • Debate Over "Unabated":
    • The term "unabated" is at the forefront of discussions at the global climate talks in Dubai, particularly regarding the transition away from fossil fuels.
    • Leaders like Sultan Al Jaber and John Kerry have emphasized the importance of phasing out "unabated" fossil fuels to combat climate change and achieve net-zero emissions by 2050.
  • Definition and Complexity:
    • "Unabated" fossil fuels refer to those emitted without significant efforts to remove greenhouse gasses, such as carbon dioxide, from oil, natural gas, and coal emissions.
    • However, there is no universally agreed-upon definition of "unabated," leading to complexities in negotiations and potential loopholes in agreements.
  • Carbon Capture and Storage (CCS):
    • The debate over "abatement" focuses on the role of carbon capture and storage (CCS) technologies in reducing emissions from fossil fuel production and use.
    • Some argue that CCS should play a central role in reducing emissions, while others remain skeptical about its effectiveness and affordability.
  • Challenges and Concerns:
    • Critics worry that relying too heavily on abatement technologies could allow continued reliance on fossil fuels and hinder the transition to renewable energy sources.
    • There are doubts about the scalability, cost-effectiveness, and practicality of CCS technologies in achieving significant emissions reductions.
  • Role of Nations and Industry:
    • The stance on abatement varies among nations and industries, with disagreements over the feasibility and necessity of transitioning away from unabated fossil fuels.The COP28 negotiations aim to address these issues and establish agreements that facilitate a meaningful transition to cleaner energy sources.

Overall, the article underscores the importance of defining and addressing the concept of "unabated" fossil fuels in climate negotiations while highlighting the complexities and challenges associated with implementing effective emission reduction strategies.
