Case Study: Resiliency Program (Miami Beach, FL)

July 23, 2024

Case StudyCulture Led DevelopmentCulture Led Development

Summary:

The Resiliency Program in Miami Beach, Florida, is a pioneering initiative designed to address the challenges posed by climate change and rising sea levels. Highlighted by the Urban Land Institute (ULI), this program focuses on enhancing the city’s infrastructure, public spaces, and community resilience through innovative design and planning strategies. Launched in the early 2010s, the program aims to safeguard Miami Beach’s economic vitality, environmental health, and quality of life for its residents. The program encompasses a wide range of projects, including the elevation of roads, installation of advanced stormwater management systems, and the construction of new seawalls and green infrastructure. One of the flagship projects is the raising of Alton Road, a crucial thoroughfare, to mitigate flooding. This project, along with the installation of pump stations and underground water storage facilities, significantly reduces the risk of flooding during heavy rainfalls and high tides.

A key objective of the Resiliency Program is to integrate sustainability into urban development. The program promotes the use of green infrastructure, such as permeable pavements and bioswales, to manage stormwater naturally. Additionally, the city has implemented strict building codes that require new developments to incorporate flood-resistant designs and materials, ensuring that future constructions are better equipped to handle the impacts of climate change. The economic impact of the Resiliency Program is substantial, as it helps protect Miami Beach’s real estate market, tourism industry, and local businesses from the adverse effects of flooding and sea-level rise. By investing in resilient infrastructure, the city attracts private investment and maintains its appeal as a premier tourist destination. The program also supports community engagement and education initiatives, encouraging residents and businesses to adopt resilient practices and contribute to the city’s long-term sustainability.

The success of the Resiliency Program is attributed to the collaboration between the City of Miami Beach, the Urban Land Institute (ULI), local stakeholders, and international experts. The ULI has recognized the program for its innovative approach and significant contribution to urban resilience, showcasing it as a model for other coastal cities facing similar challenges.
